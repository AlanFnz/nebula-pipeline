# Changelog

## 0.133.0 · 2026-11-24

Radial bloom adds an optional nested gradient pattern. Edit the disc spacing and twist, gradient focus, palette colors, color cycle and radial diffusion in Object.

## 0.132.0 · 2026-11-24

Montage gives music markers their own lane above the clips. The playhead, selected cuts and insertion point have distinct shapes. The block timing inspector groups the selected block, source In, duration, speed and Apply timing.

The step sequencer aligns note names with their row label. A blue focus outline distinguishes keyboard navigation from the selected and playing steps.

## 0.131.0 · 2026-11-24

Montage Material shows compact thumbnails as you scroll, names on up to two lines and usage counts. Hover over a row for its full name. Dragging a source marks the insertion point on the timeline. Replace block identifies the target before you confirm the source range.

## 0.130.0 · 2026-11-24

The synthesizer mixer aligns channel levels, Mute, Solo and the three EQ bands for side-by-side editing. Compact dials leave room for gain and frequency values at 1280 × 800. Chimes keeps its independent high-pass control below the EQ.

## 0.129.0 · 2026-11-24

Numeric inspector controls align each value with its Restore action. In a narrow inspector the slider shares a second line with the value, and Animate stays beside the label. When editing intensity across clips, the selected range appears beside the clip count.

## 0.128.0 · 2026-11-23

Studio Graphite now extends to the effect list and preset previews. The list highlights the selected effect and shows its processing order and bypass state. The preview window groups Before, Preview and Intensity, and distinguishes temporary adjustments from effects already applied.

## 0.127.0 · 2026-11-22

Studio Graphite now extends to the timeline and playback controls. A time ruler and a shared playhead color make cuts easier to locate. Prepared frames appear in sage, with an amber hatch while a gap is being filled. Preview resolution, coverage and activity share a row when space allows.

## 0.126.0 · 2026-11-22

The composition header, monitor and inspector use the Studio Graphite palette. Document actions and canvas settings have distinct rows, with clearer headings and keyboard focus in the inspector.

## 0.125.1 · 2026-11-22

When editing intensity across multiple clips, the inspector shows how many clips it affects and where the other controls apply.

## 0.125.0 · 2026-11-22

Edit effect intensity across selected clips. Drag to preserve their differences or enter a shared percentage. Each edit has one undo step.

## 0.124.1 · 2026-11-22

Preview playback keeps the first soundtrack note continuous when starting a prepared loop at low frame rates.

## 0.124.0

The editor can load a local study catalogue. Projects, library preferences and render settings keep their existing formats. A standalone installation starts with an editable shape.
